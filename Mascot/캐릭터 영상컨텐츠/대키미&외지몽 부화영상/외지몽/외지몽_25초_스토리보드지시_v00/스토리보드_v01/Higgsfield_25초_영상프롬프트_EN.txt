Create a 25-second, 16:9, soft stylized 3D hatching film featuring ONE Oejimong baby. A continuous, physically connected action in one scene, viewed by a fixed low front camera.

REFERENCE ROLES
01_BABY_FRONT: authoritative character identity, proportions, color, blush, forelocks, hands and feet.
02_BABY_TURNAROUND: supporting side/back structure only. It depicts the SAME single baby, not multiple characters.
03_EGG_TURNAROUND: egg shape and decoration only. Use its FRONT view for the opening. Do not reproduce reference-sheet grids, labels or multiple eggs.

IDENTITY
Match the approved bright-green baby puppy alien: huge head, tiny chubby torso, very short limbs, short uniform plush fur, floppy rounded ears, two smooth unfurred antenna stems with round balls, and exactly two tiny narrow round-ended crown forelocks. Glossy black eyes each carry a large cream FOUR-point star, a small gray sparkle and a tiny pale diamond. Cream muzzle and belly, small black puppy nose, happy little tongue, small round translucent rose-pink/red blush. Rounded mitten hands and oval feet with almost no visible digit grooves. Visible palms/soles have one main cream pad plus THREE small oval pads, smooth and hairless. One plump curved puppy tail with a small rounded cream tip. Confident, curious and delighted to meet the viewer.

EGG AND SCENE
Start with only ONE intact UPRIGHT egg, taller than wide, with no baby visible outside. Warm cream matte shell, one gently tilted milky-mint paw mark at the front center, made of one main pad and three small ovals, plus scattered rounded FOUR-point mint stars. No ribbon. Keep the same egg silhouette, colors and decoration as the reference. Warm pale cream seamless floor/background and soft diffused light. Leave several small baby steps between the egg and the lens. The baby is inside this egg, not spawned beside it.

TIMELINE
0–3s: The intact upright egg rocks twice, pauses briefly, and develops a small crack near its upper front.
3–6s: A small upper opening lifts. The baby's eyes and nose peek out; most of the body remains hidden. It blinks, notices the camera, and becomes visibly delighted.
6–9s: Both small hands brace the opening. It eagerly pushes its head and upper chest out. The floppy ears and round antenna balls gently follow; the two little forelocks become visible. The lower body is still inside.
9–12s: As it leans too eagerly forward, the shell opens into a few large rounded pieces. Both front paws touch the floor first, followed by a soft tummy flop. The rump remains slightly raised, hind feet trail briefly, and the tail comes out behind. No backward fall, sitting fall or violent impact.
12–15s: It lifts its head, meets the viewer's eyes, wags its tail first, then gets up on its tiny legs with a happy expression. Large shell pieces stay behind it.
15–20s: It toddles into an eager short-legged run straight toward the stationary camera. One little wobble is recovered naturally. Ears and antennae bounce with a slight delay, round balls retain their shape, and blush becomes just slightly warmer.
20–22s: It tries to stop near the lens but its black nose gives the camera one gentle BOOP. A tiny contact sound and slight camera nudge; it blinks once. No injury, hard collision or paws striking the lens.
22–25s: It takes half a step back so both eyes, the forelocks and antenna balls fit in the close framing. It smiles with warm direct eye contact and a little tongue visible. Its tail keeps wagging. Hold this friendly ending.

CAMERA AND CONTINUITY
No cuts, orbit, tracking, digital zoom or sudden reframing. The baby becomes larger because it physically approaches the lens. Keep the action centered. Only the gentle nose touch causes a tiny camera movement. Maintain one baby, one original egg, consistent anatomy and stable character design. Shell pieces remain at the hatching location; they do not reassemble or follow the baby.

AUDIO
Only quiet diegetic shell cracking, soft paw contacts, faint fur movement and the tiny nose-contact sound. NO dialogue, cooing, laughter, barking, narration, singing or music. A separate character voice will be designed later.

AVOID
Horizontal or spherical egg; changed mint/cream patterns; ribbon; five-point stars; duplicate characters; premature full-body reveal; skipped emergence or tummy-flop stages; extra limbs or antennae; enlarged toes or human fingers; pointed crown hair; puffed cheeks; long fur; shrinking or spiral cat tail; violent shell explosion; distress crying; text, labels, grids, logos or watermarks.
